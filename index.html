How It Works

Three steps to your flight board

1

Search or tap Nearby

Find any airport by city name, airport name, or IATA code. Or let BoardPass detect your nearest airport automatically.

2

View the board

See live departures and arrivals. Sort by time, flight, city, or status. Filter to find exactly the flight you need.

3

Track your flight

Tap any flight for gate, terminal, and baggage details. Add it to your watchlist to keep tracking it from anywhere in the app.
